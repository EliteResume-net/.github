## About EliteResume

EliteResume is an AI-powered career platform that helps job seekers create ATS-friendly resumes, tailor applications to specific roles, identify missing keywords, improve resume content, generate cover letters, and organize their job search in one workspace.

Our goal is to make professional resume tools accessible to everyone—from students and career changers to experienced professionals and executives.

## What EliteResume Offers

* **AI Resume and CV Builder**
  Create professional resumes with AI-assisted summaries, skills, and achievement-focused bullet points.

* **ATS Resume Checker**
  Review resume structure, readability, formatting, content quality, and ATS compatibility.

* **ATS CV Checker**
  Analyze CVs using terminology and conventions relevant to UK and European job markets.

* **Resume Keyword Scanner**
  Compare a resume with a job description to find matched and missing keywords.

* **Resume Optimizer**
  Identify evidence gaps, weak bullet points, and opportunities to better align a resume with a target role.

* **AI Cover Letter Generator**
  Create editable, job-specific cover letters based on professional experience and target positions.

* **23 ATS-Friendly Templates**
  Choose from professional resume templates available across all EliteResume plans.

* **Resume Examples**
  Explore role-specific resume examples, summaries, skills, keywords, and achievement-focused bullet points.

* **Job Application Tracker**
  Organize job opportunities, tailored resumes, scans, and application progress.

## How It Works

1. **Build or upload** your resume.
2. **Tailor** it to a real job description.
3. **Scan** it for ATS, keyword, and content issues.
4. **Improve** weak sections using focused recommendations.
5. **Export or share** your completed resume.
6. **Track** each application in one workspace.

## Our Principles

* Practical guidance over generic advice
* Clear and transparent product limits
* ATS-friendly structure without keyword stuffing
* AI assistance grounded in the user’s real experience
* Privacy-conscious handling of resume information
* Accessible tools for job seekers at every career stage
